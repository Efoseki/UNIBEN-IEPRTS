How the system works

From observation to tracked resolution

1. Report

Describe the infrastructure or environmental problem, its location and severity.

2. Add Evidence

Upload photographs and, where available, geographical coordinates.

3. Track

Use the unique reference number to monitor assignment, action and progress.

4. Resolve

Responsible personnel record actions taken and update the report through closure.

Infrastructure

Campus infrastructure problems

Buildings, electricity, water supply, roads, walkways, lecture facilities, hostels, ICT, laboratories, security facilities, drainage infrastructure and related defects.

Environment

Campus environmental problems

Waste disposal, cleanliness, flooding, pollution, sanitation, pests, environmental hazards, offensive odours, erosion, landscaping and related concerns.
